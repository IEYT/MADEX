Manual Dexterity Test



This is a manual dexterity test designed to evaluate your fine motor skills. I made it for my Science Fair. You will have to type 20 random characters; a-z and 0-9. This is considered a manual dexterity test because it means a test on how accurately, fast, and consistently you can do stuff with fingers.

Instructions: To assess, use assessment before doing an activity and after and compare. Good luck!
