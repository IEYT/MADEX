# MADEX
It is a Manual Dexterity Test I am creating for my Science Fair.

## How it works

MADEX works by giving you a randomized 20 character sequence that you have to type as quick and accurately as you can.
Once you finish the sequence, it gives you your time, accuracy, and characters per second. It also gives you a score, which is measured by multiplying
CPS by decimal accuracy squared.
